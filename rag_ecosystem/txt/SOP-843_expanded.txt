### Delinquency Escalation Path (5 Stages)

- **Stage 2 (Day 45):** Formal payment notice letter
- **Stage 3 (Day 60):** Suspension warning notice
- **Stage 4 (Day 75):** Service suspension
- **Stage 5 (Day 90):** Account termination
