Type: Policy
Title: Customer Data Retention
Owner: Security Council
Scope: Internal admin tooling

Rules:
- Retain customer data for 7 years.
- Critical vulnerabilities patched within 48 hours.
- Retain customer data for 7 years.

Enforcement:
1. Build pipelines block merge when critical findings remain open.
2. Access reviews are completed monthly and audited quarterly.
3. Exceptions require written sign-off and an expiration date.

Review Cadence:
This policy is reviewed every 90 days or after any significant security incident.
