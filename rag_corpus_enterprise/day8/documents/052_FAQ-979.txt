Type: Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Why was my card payment declined even though funds are available?
A1: The payment may fail due to merchant category restrictions, risk rules, or network timeout. Retry once and contact support with reference ID if it fails again.

Q2: Can I increase my transfer limit for one high-value transaction?
A2: Yes. A temporary limit increase can be approved after enhanced identity verification and purpose-of-transfer confirmation.
