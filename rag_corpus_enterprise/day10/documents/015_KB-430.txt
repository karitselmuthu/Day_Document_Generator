Title: Filing Health Insurance Claim

Overview:
This article explains the end-to-end flow for policyholders and support agents. Follow
the sequence exactly to avoid validation failures in the downstream processing queue.

Steps:
1. Login with registered credentials.
2. Navigate to Claims -> Submit New Claim.
3. Upload required documents.
4. Claims processed within 7 business days.

Troubleshooting:
- If the portal is unavailable, use the assisted-call workflow with ticket tagging.
- For urgent claims, mark severity as high and attach hospitalization records.
- For urgent claims, mark severity as high and attach hospitalization records.
